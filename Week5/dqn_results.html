Key findings:
• Slow ε decay matters most — more exploration time before committing is the single biggest factor.
• Self-play outperforms random and minimax opponents — diverse training situations help generalization.
• LR=5e-4 is the sweet spot — too high overshoots (38%), too low underfits (45%) in 4k episodes.
• DQN plateaus around 50–56% — the DQN vs tabular gap on a game this small is expected and meaningful.
