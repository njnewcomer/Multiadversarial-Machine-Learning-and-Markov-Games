Key PG findings:
• LR=1e-3 wins at 62.2% — outperforms best DQN (54.3%) on this metric.
• Whitened returns (57.5%) — normalizing returns to zero mean / unit std is the second-best trick.
• Mean baseline (default, 62.2%) — subtracting mean return reduces variance effectively.
• Entropy bonus hurts — adding entropy regularization degrades performance here; the game already provides enough exploration via the stochastic policy.
• Most configs collapse to 38.2% — this is a pathological fixed point where the policy agrees with minimax on all states where minimax says U (which is 38% of states). Getting out of this requires the right LR.
